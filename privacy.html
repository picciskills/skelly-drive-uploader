Privacy Policy — Skelly Drive Uploader

Last updated: November 26, 2026.

Skelly Drive Uploader is an internal tool used only by its owner. It accesses Google Drive solely to list, upload and replace the owner's own files in the owner's own folders.

No data is collected from, shared with or sold to any third party. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not transferred to anyone, and is stored only on the owner's own machines. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
